Everything you reach for all day now lives where your eyes already go: your Dock.

Crohniq Dock is a glass widget bar for macOS. It sits gracefully above your Dock, blends into your desktop and shows you what matters right now – your next meeting, the weather and the song that's playing.

YOUR DAY, AT A GLANCE
• Calendar and Reminders – next event, daily agenda, check off tasks in place
• Mail – your latest messages, optionally only from people you know
• Weather with forecast and rain radar
• Clocks for any time zone, digital or classic analog

FOCUS THAT FEELS GOOD
• Pomodoro, timer, stopwatch and focus sessions
• Hydration reminder with a daily goal
• Progress tracker for your goals
• Quick notes, ready the moment you need them

YOUR MAC, FASTER
• App launcher, app switcher and one-click "Quit Apps"
• Workspaces: open Office, Development or your own setup – every app lands exactly where it belongs
• Finder, Downloads, Dropbox, bookmarks and Apple Shortcuts
• Copy & paste history for text and images
• Screenshots with history, voice memos, emoji and a color picker
• Volume, quick toggles, battery, CPU, memory and disk
• A trash can worth looking at

ASK WITHOUT SWITCHING APPS
Ask Apple Intelligence right from the bar – or send your question to Claude or ChatGPT with a single keystroke.

LISTS THAT UNFOLD
Hover over a widget and it unrolls: recent documents, copied items, recently played songs, an app's windows. One click and you're there.

MADE FOR YOUR STYLE
Pick from more than 50 widgets, organize them into profiles, and tune size, spacing, opacity and glass tint. Photos and panorama wallpapers from your library make the bar truly yours.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Crohniq Dock reads your calendar and mail only on your Mac and never sends them anywhere. A permissions assistant explains every access before you grant it – and you can revoke any of them at any time.
